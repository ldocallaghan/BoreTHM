# Entry 5: measured 16B mud rheology

These are development comparisons on the frozen 16A and 16B samples. The calculation is entry 4's working calculation (entry 1 with D5, entry 3 and entry 4's rotary heat). For 16B it uses the S2 mud, which fills a missing PV or YP from the nearest measurement in the same fluid system, where the scoreboard's original S1 mud used the reference programme mud (PV 27 cP, YP 28 lbf/100 ft²). The donors are listed in [`mud-revision-ledger.csv`](../../../forge16b/s2/mud-revision-ledger.csv). 16A is unchanged. PV is referenced to 26.7 °C, the film multiplier is 1.0, exposure is cumulative with the one-hour floor, and 16A and 16B use the NC50 and EIS connection scenarios. Nothing is fitted to a temperature, pressure or residual. The historical trial insulation conductivity remains, as disclosed in the development log.

`circulation.csv`, `run-in.csv` and `scoreboard.csv` come from the frozen scoreboard functions, run by `../score.py`. `rotary-heat-applied.csv` lists the rotary heat applied to every circulation sample, as in entry 4. `../compare.py` adds `comparison.csv`, which sets each scoreboard row beside the legacy baseline and the working calculation, and `assembly-changes.csv`, which gives the change in bit and return temperature from the working calculation by assembly. `manifest.json` records the commits and source hashes.

All 467 circulation cases and all nine originally available run-in events completed, with the same sample counts as both reference calculations. All 109 16B samples received their S2 mud. Bit temperatures of samples whose mud is unchanged differ from entry 4's by at most 0.0006 °C, from the exact rotary-source scaling added after entry 4 was scored. The declaration, predictions and interpretation are in the [development log](../../../../docs/science/model-development.md). Source observations retain the attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms stated in the [16A](../../../forge16a/README.md) and [16B](../../../forge16b/README.md) archives.
