# Entry 4: rotary heat

These are development comparisons on the frozen 16A and 16B samples. The calculation is the working calculation (entry 1 with D5 and entry 3) with measured surface rotary power added as heat: wall friction released into the annulus fluid along the string, and the remainder as work at the bit, split by the soft-string torque balance in `src/borethm/rotary_heat.py`. PV is referenced to 26.7 °C, the film multiplier is 1.0, exposure is cumulative with the one-hour floor, and 16A and 16B use the NC50 and EIS connection scenarios. The friction factors come from the off-bottom rotating torque; nothing is fitted to a temperature, pressure or residual. The historical trial insulation conductivity remains, as disclosed in the development log.

`circulation.csv`, `run-in.csv` and `scoreboard.csv` come from the frozen scoreboard functions, run by `../score.py`. `rotary-heat-applied.csv` lists the friction factor, its basis, the friction and bit heat, and whether the friction was capped, for every circulation sample. `../compare.py` adds `comparison.csv`, which sets each scoreboard row beside the legacy baseline and the working calculation, and `assembly-changes.csv`, which gives the change in bit and return temperature from the working calculation, summarised by assembly. `manifest.json` records the commits and source hashes.

All 467 circulation cases and all nine originally available run-in events completed, with the same sample counts as both reference calculations. The run-in starting profiles receive no rotary heat. The declaration, predictions and interpretation are in the [development log](../../../../docs/science/model-development.md).
