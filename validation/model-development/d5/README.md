# Assembly input audit

The 248 rows in `components.csv` cover the inputs to all 22 development-well assemblies. `components.json` contains the same records for the study adapter. Rebuild both with `python validation/model-development/d5/build_audit.py`. Original report cells and the earlier adopted values are separate columns. A blank reported dimension remains null. An explicitly documented transfer between assemblies remains identified as a transfer.

`reported_bore_below_2in` lists original small bores; `earlier_bore_below_2in` also identifies filled values in the earlier implementation. `tool` identifies motors and instrument or valve components. The `source` column gives a report member and hash or an End of Well Report page. The original extracts are licensed CC BY 4.0, with attribution in the [16A](../../forge16a/README.md) and [16B](../../forge16b/README.md) archives.

All nine 16A bit IDs were blank. The earlier adapter assigned 1.25 in. The BHA 18 motor ID was also blank; eight other motor IDs were reported as 1.25 in. The short BHA 18 PUK sub has a reported 1.25-inch bore, which remains in the passage calculation. The BHA 23 MWD tool and BHA 38/39 PUK subs have unresolved bores. A separately declared, authorised 3.25-inch thermal-only assumption now permits their calculation. Their recorded and hydraulic IDs remain unknown, and no internal hydraulic loss is added.

The trial rows retain the earlier transfer of Figure 90 component data to EOW 18–20, with its limitations visible. The motor is separated from the remaining compact assembly for D5. Its 35-ft length follows the original Figure 90 convention; the 41.87-ft Appendix B discrepancy remains unresolved. Other trial segment lengths and the fitted insulation conductivity remain fixed. This separation does not convert the transferred table into an own-assembly measurement.

## Motor curves

A motor curve must match the make, diameter and configuration. A candidate near-match was verified in Turbo Drill Industries (2023), *CAMScout Motor Specifications*, PDF page 19: [manufacturer catalogue](https://scoutdownhole.com/uploads/scout-downhole/CAMScoutMotorSpecifications73123.pdf#page=19). It lists a 6½-inch, 7/8, 5.7-stage CAMScout mud-lubricated motor and 150 psi off-bottom pressure. The field record identifies a Scout fixed-bend motor for 16A BHA 18, but does not establish this bearing/configuration match or the curve's test flow and density. The other 16A records do not establish a complete motor-to-sheet mapping. The 16B illustrated tables identify TiTAN 22 assemblies in several runs; no verified matching no-load curve has been located. No curve from another configuration is adopted.

Every motor therefore has the qualification **motor loss unknown** in this correction. Its additional internal off-bottom pressure is zero pending a matching curve. This is an omitted loss, not evidence of a loss-free motor. A verified curve can use the existing relation `dp = dp_ref (rho/rho_ref) (Q/Q_ref)^2`, with its reference conditions recorded. The existing reported-differential face heat remains unchanged.
