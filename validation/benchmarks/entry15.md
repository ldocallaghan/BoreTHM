# Entry 15 radial recovery verification

The [Well 4 calculation](entry15_recovery.py) uses the Kutasov and Eppelbaum (2014) inputs and tolerance in the [benchmark definition](README.md#venezuelan-well-4). Table 1 gives calculated recovery fractions from their analytical model. The paper does not specify the liquid thermal capacity or an exchange coefficient for the static BoreTHM node. It describes the liner as an assumed installation. The comparison uses a prescribed cold wall during circulation and nominal liquid-water volumetric heat capacity and conductivity (4.18 MJ/(m³ K) and 0.60 W/(m K)) as comparison assumptions. The conduction floor uses Nu = 4.36 with the hydraulic diameter. Both the full-hole and liner-excluded liquid capacities are reported in the [radial-grid results](entry15-well4.csv). A separate high-film limit illustrates sensitivity to the exchange assumption. It has no standing as a measured or sourced film value.

The conduction-floor result exceeds the ±0.05 recovery fraction tolerance for both circulation durations with full-hole capacity. Excluding liner volume still exceeds tolerance after 36 h of circulation. This prevents progressing to event scoring under the adoption rule. Radial-grid comparisons address discretisation of these local cases only.

The [Khaled calculation](entry15_khaled.py) treats the pipe as present in the static annulus and prescribes a 114 °C wall for the reported 3.5 h exposure. These assumptions initialise the rock and fluid for a conditional comparison. The [results](entry15-khaled.csv) give 114.7 °C at five minutes and 207.7 °C at 24 hours, compared with 119 °C and 220 °C in Table 3. The case has no pass tolerance.

The [Brown pump-off case](README.md#brown-et-al-pump-off-connection) was reclassified before calculating a recovery result. The source gives a plotted history and omits the initial fluid and rock profiles and the fitted Fourier number for the effective formation radius. An independently reproducible pump-off threshold cannot be calculated from those inputs.
