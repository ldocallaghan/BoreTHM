# Paired coverage clarification

1 November 2026, before Pason window-pressure access.

The registered comparison contains three calculations: original, working entry 10 and secondary entry 8. In the protocol's coverage paragraph, "both models" means all three registered calculations. A paired window has complete registered inputs, convergence in all three calculations and a complete pressure target. Every error and contrast in a cohort uses identical window IDs. An event without a paired window remains unavailable.

Each equipment scenario has its own paired cohort. The common cohort is the intersection of those cohorts across all four scenarios. Window counts and event counts are reported separately. Calculation-specific convergence remains visible even where another calculation fails.

All 186 primary seeded solves converged before access, so this clarification changes no primary model-only cohort. It changes no input, sampling rule, exclusion, equation or measure in [the registered protocol](protocol.md).
