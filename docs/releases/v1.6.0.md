# v1.6.0: pressure losses, the rock response and field comparisons

Released on 29 October 2026 as [`v1.6.0`](https://github.com/ldocallaghan/BoreTHM/releases/tag/v1.6.0), at commit `c0eac6b393e4c3252b24938185366b8e4c84d318`.

## Changes to the calculation

The circulation model calculates pressure losses component by component: drill pipe with optional tool-joint restrictions, serial assembly components with their recorded bores, supplied tool curves, the bit nozzles and the surface equipment. Each loss heats the stream in which it occurs. A `HydraulicProgramme` supplies these inputs in Python or a site file. The [physical-model guide](../science/models.md) describes the terms.

The rock around the well responds as an infinite cylinder to a step in wall temperature, calculated from the exact solution. It replaces the logarithmic approximation in the v1.6.0 calculation and lowers the rock resistance by about 5–8% for exposures from one hour to 30 days.

`circulating_temperatures` accepts `annulus_heat`, a heat source per unit length in the annulus. `rotary_heat.partition` divides measured surface rotary power between wall friction and bit work with a soft-string torque balance. Site evaluations do not apply it.

Each change was declared with its sources and predictions before it was scored against FORGE 16A and 16B, and an independent review upheld the decisions. The [development log](../science/model-development.md) records the declarations, the scores and the review. The [validation guide](../science/validation.md) adds the FORGE 16A and 16B whole-well comparisons and the 56-32 standpipe-pressure comparison.

## Changes to existing behaviour

### Default results

The default calculation is v1.6.0, so results from `evaluate`, `circulating_temperatures`, `hydraulics` and the commands change. The five built-in sites keep their status:

| Site | Status | Flow | Bit temperature | Target cases with an open drilling interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Soultz | NO-GO | 40 → 45 L/s | 197.0 → 196.3 °C | 8 of 9 → 2 of 9 |
| United Downs | NO-GO | 50 → 40 L/s | 205.7 → 239.4 °C | 2 of 4 → 1 of 4 |
| Larderello | CONDITIONAL | 30 L/s | 66.4 → 68.7 °C | 5 of 5 |
| Pannonian | NO-GO | 40 L/s | 184.7 → 195.2 °C | 5 of 5 |
| Newberry | GO | 30 kg/s | 89.0 → 92.1 °C | 6 of 6 |

At United Downs the pump limit sets the flow, and the added losses lower it. The added annulus losses raise the circulating pressure and lower the upper drilling bound. Pannonian's run-in tool check now fails; with the logarithmic rock term it still passes. The rows are in `tests/golden/v160_site_table.json`.

### The previous calculation

`hydraulics_model='v1.5.0'`, passed to `evaluate`, `circulating_temperatures` or `hydraulics`, or set in a programme, gives the previous calculation with the logarithmic rock term. The v1.0 calculation, the circulation modes before v1.3.4 and the archived v1.3.4 and v1.3.6 studies use it unless a programme is given, so they reproduce their published tables. The breakout-timing and design-comparison guides state this basis.

### Benchmarks

Benchmark dataset 1.0 is unchanged and is reproduced by the v1.5.0 calculation. The UT Austin values from the v1.6.0 calculation are 0.1–2.0 °C warmer and are stored in their own golden file.
