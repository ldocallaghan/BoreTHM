# v1.5.1: faster evaluations with identical results

Release candidate prepared on 24 October 2026. Tagging and publication are pending.

The circulation model reuses fluid and geometry lookups within each step.
Identical circulation solves are also reused within one assessment or study
trial, with independent copies of each result. The cache is cleared when the
operation ends, including exceptional exits.

On the reference Ryzen 7 5700G, the five built-in assessments take about
20 to 346 seconds with an existing water-property cache, down from 30 to
610 seconds. The [performance page](../maintenance/performance.md#v151-measurements)
records three runs per site and the measured contribution of each change.
The shared table-cell-search prototype was removed after exact interpolation
tests failed on macOS. Water-property interpolation is unchanged.

United Downs peak resident memory increased from 122.82 to 187.83 MiB.
The Soultz coating trial remained about 51.5 seconds. A single Newberry run
under Pyodide in Node.js fell from 51.34 to 29.69 seconds and reproduced its
original full export exactly.

## Study caches and citation

The source fingerprint changes. As after every release, rerunning a study
recomputes saved trials whose fingerprint belongs to the earlier code.
Previously saved scientific results and reference files are unchanged.

The software's [all-versions DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23549104)
and the author's ORCID iD are preserved. Benchmark dataset 1.0 keeps its
[separate citation](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23565448).
