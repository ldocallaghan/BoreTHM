# v1.8.0: the FORGE development configuration as default physics, with generic tool-loss allowances

Planned for 6 November 2026.

## What changed

Every result calculated with the package defaults now differs from v1.7.0. The calculation that the development log compared with FORGE 16A and 16B, described as the FORGE development configuration in the [v1.7.0 record](v1.7.0.md), becomes the default, and downhole tools gain generic pressure-loss allowances.

The defaults are named sets of settings. `physics='v1.8.0'` is the default, and `physics='v1.7.0'` restores every previous value:

| Setting | `physics='v1.8.0'` (default) | `physics='v1.7.0'` |
|---|---|---|
| Annulus pressure and flow regime | Herschel-Bulkley slot model of Founargiotakis et al. (2008), with the transition band of Erge et al. (2015) | Bingham plastic with the apparent-viscosity criterion |
| Annulus heat transfer | Gnielinski's annulus correlation on the plastic viscosity, with laminar values when the flow regime is laminar | Dittus-Boelter on the plastic viscosity, in every regime |
| Mud with only plastic viscosity and yield point | Converted to a power law with zero yield stress | Bingham plastic |
| Pipe friction | Colebrook on a smooth wall, `friction='colebrook'`, `roughness=0.` | Blasius |
| Ordinary drill-pipe joint transitions | Conical at 11.5° included angle in the bore, with the coefficients of Crane Technical Paper 410; the annulus stays abrupt | Abrupt |
| Downhole tool losses | Generic allowances for the tools named on the string | Zero unless entered |
| Motor pressure drop | No-load drop plus differential; the default no-load figure is zero, with a warning, until a figure is entered | Differential only |

The argument is accepted by `evaluate`, `circulating_temperatures` and `hydraulics`, as `--physics` by `borethm-evaluate` and `borethm-sites`, and as `physics` in the `[programme]` table of a site file. A setting given explicitly overrides its row, and `hydraulics_model='v1.5.0'` always uses the v1.7.0 values. The result records the settings used under `physics`.

Tools are declared with `DownholeTool`, on a `StringSegment` through `tools` or in a site file through `[[programme.bha_tools]]`. The [new-site guide](../guides/new-site.md#default-physics-and-downhole-tools) gives the format.

## Changes to results

**Every default result changes.** To obtain v1.7.0 results, pass `physics='v1.7.0'`. With that argument the v1.7.0 golden tables, site exports and benchmark values reproduce, as the verification table records.

Each site was also evaluated with one setting at a time changed from v1.7.0, which gives the cause of each change:

- **Pump pressure.** The conical joints account for most of the fall at the deep sites: 5.4 MPa at Soultz, 6.4 MPa at United Downs at 45 L/s and 4.1 MPa at the Pannonian Basin. The annulus model lowers it by 1.1 to 2.4 MPa at these sites and Larderello. The tool allowances raise it by 0.4 to 1.2 MPa at every site. Colebrook friction changes it by 0.2 MPa or less at these sites, and raises it by 0.4 MPa at Newberry, which circulates water.
- **United Downs flow.** The conical joints alone raise the selected flow from 40 to 45 L/s, because the same pump limit then allows more flow. The higher flow is the main cause of the 13 °C fall at the bit and the rise in return temperature, and it raises the pump pressure above its v1.7.0 value.
- **Bit temperature.** At Soultz and the Pannonian Basin, the conical joints alone lower the bit temperature by 2.2 to 2.9 °C and the tool allowances alone raise it by 1.3 to 1.5 °C. These changes have the same sign as the change in pump pressure, which is consistent with the frictional heat added to the fluid. The exception is Larderello, where the annulus model alone accounts for the 22.4 °C fall. The annulus there is laminar under both presets. At v1.7.0 its heat transfer used a turbulent correlation regardless, and at v1.8.0 it follows the laminar regime, so less heat is expected to reach the fluid from the rock. This regime depends on the zero yield stress described under "Limitations".
- **Fluid limits.** The upper drilling limit rises mainly through the annulus model, which lowers the equivalent circulating density, the annulus pressure loss expressed as added fluid density, from 0.157 to 0.137 SG at Soultz and from 0.143 to 0.086 SG at Larderello. At United Downs the higher flow outweighs this, so the upper limit falls. The lower drilling and trip limits change by less than 0.003 SG at Soultz and the Pannonian Basin, mainly through the conical joints. At Larderello the annulus model alone moves them by 0.006 and 0.009 SG, and at United Downs the conical joints alone move them by 0.010 and 0.004 SG.
- **Tool limit.** The check on the 200 °C tool limit changes at Soultz and the Pannonian Basin. At v1.7.0 running in needed continuous circulation. At v1.8.0 a staged schedule is found, and either the annulus model or the conical joints alone gives this result. Both sites remain NO-GO because no fluid density satisfies the pressure limits. United Downs still exceeds the limit at the bit while drilling, at 226 °C, and during run-in.

## Basis

Each part of the default physics comes from a published source, and each was chosen after comparison with FORGE 16A and 16B. These wells therefore test the implementation and do not independently validate the choice.

- **Annulus and rheology:** [entry 8](../science/model-development.md#31-october-2026-entry-8-score), using Founargiotakis et al. (2008), Erge et al. (2015) and Gnielinski's annulus correlation.
- **Pipe friction:** [entry 10](../science/model-development.md#31-october-2026-entry-10-sourced-pressure-options), using Colebrook's equation on a smooth wall.
- **Joint transitions:** [entry 13](../science/model-development.md#4-november-2026-entry-13-tapered-drill-pipe-joints), using Crane Technical Paper 410 with a published internal-upset taper.
- **Tool allowances:** [entry 14](../science/model-development.md#5-november-2026-entry-14-tool-loss-allowances-on-the-adopted-calculation), using published specification sheets.

Entry 14 scored the allowances against the frozen 467-sample FORGE record, with the defaults and predictions declared before the score. Review then found that its assignment gave the HALO rotary steerable assemblies of 16B no MWD (measurement while drilling) pulser, although their BHA sheets print one. The [amendment](../science/model-development.md#entry-14-amendment-6-november-2026-tool-assignment) corrected this from those sheets, after the first scores had been seen, with new predictions declared before the rescore. The tool table, the rule and the defaults did not change. The table below gives the amended scores, which moved in mixed directions:

|---|---:|---:|

| 16A hydraulic power bias, kW | −288 | −230 |

Bias is the model minus the measurement. Pressure agreement improves on both wells. Five 16A assemblies with cold bit biases come closer, and four with warm bit biases move further, as do the 16B warm biases. The next tests are the unseen-well thermal comparisons with FORGE 56-32, 58-32 and 78B-32, each declared before its targets are read.

## Tool-loss defaults

The allowances are generic figures for a class of tool and a collar size. They describe no particular tool in any well. **Enter the maker's figure for the actual tool when you have it.** A tool's loss is ΔP_ref × (ρ/ρ_ref) × (Q/Q_ref)^n, and its heat enters the fluid at the tool's depth.

Each usable published figure is converted to water at the band's reference flow, and the default is the median across source families. MWD means measurement while drilling.

| Class | Collar band, in | Reference flow, US gal/min | Default in water, psi | Exponent | Range of sources, psi | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| MWD pulser | 4.75 | 250 | 149.9 | 1.61 | 107.6–208.5 | |
| MWD pulser | 6.75 | 600 | 187.7 | 2.0 | 87.0–293.9 | |
| MWD pulser | 8 | 800 | 185.0 | 1.82 | 94.3–309.2 | |
| MWD pulser | 9.5 | 1,000 | 100.0 | 1.0 | | One source |
| Rotary steerable | 4.75 | 250 | 69.0 | 2 | 35.9–102.0 | |
| Rotary steerable | 6.75 | 600 | 91.8 | 2 | 53.6–130 | |
| Rotary steerable | 8 | 800 | 0 | | | No source; warning |
| Rotary steerable | 9.5 | 1,000 | 32.2 | 2 | | One source |
| Mud motor at no load | All | | 0 | | | No figure with a stated flow; warning |
| Filter sub, float sub | All | | 0 | | | No source; warning |

The motor sheets retrieved print one no-load or off-bottom figure for a whole flow range, so they are recorded in the table and not used. The [table's README](../../src/borethm/data/tool-losses-README.md) gives each source and the limits of the figures. Most come from marketing and technical data sheets, and none states a mud rheology.

The reference programme of every built-in site and of a new site file now declares a 6¾-in MWD pulser allowance. This allowance is larger than the single published curve those programmes used at v1.7.0, so the tool term of the pressure budget rises at every site.

## Limitations

- **The 16A pressure gap.** The model still reads low in all nine 16A assemblies, by 355–1,673 psi. The motorless 16B assemblies read low by 6–92 psi. These wells were used in development, and that figure follows a correction found once the first scores had been seen.
- **The shallow 16B gap.** In ten shallow 16B windows with no ordinary drill pipe, the allowances account for 12–13% of the excess at 216–238 US gal/min, leaving 163–220 psi, and 23–26% at 423–492 US gal/min, leaving 308–353 psi. All of these windows are on assemblies with a motor. At 572–573 US gal/min, on two HALO assemblies, the generic allowances exceed the excess and the model reads 22–25 psi above the measurement. The ten windows cannot separate flow from assembly. The source of the remainder is unknown, and no term was added for it.
- **The regime depends on the yield stress.** The zero yield stress used for a mud reporting only plastic viscosity and yield point was chosen after FORGE 16A and 16B had been examined, so those wells do not test it. In entry 7, a yield stress at the 10-second gel made the 16A bit temperature 4–19 °C colder. Enter low-shear readings when you have them. Larderello shows the size of the effect on a site with laminar flow, as described under "Changes to results".
- **Zero defaults.** A motor, filter sub or float sub without an entered figure adds no pressure loss, and the result carries a warning.

The [limitations page](../science/limitations.md) gives the full list.
